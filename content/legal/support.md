This page is how to get help with Stablepass. It is the support page listed on the App Store and Google Play. You do not need to be signed in to use it, and you do not need the app installed.

## 1. How to get in touch

Email hello@stablepass.co. It is a monitored mailbox and it is the only support address for Stablepass — there is no phone line and no live chat.

Write from the email address your account uses where you can. Stablepass identifies an account by the address a message arrives from, and will not confirm to anyone whether a particular address has an account, so a message from a different address takes longer to act on.

It helps to include:

- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- the horse, trainer or update involved, if it is about a particular one
- your device and whether you were on the app or the website

Stablepass aims to reply within five business days.

## 2. Common things

### Signing in

Stablepass signs you in with your email address and password. If you have forgotten your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and follow the link that arrives by email.

Your account can be signed in on one device at a time. Signing in somewhere new signs the previous device out, which is deliberate — it protects the account rather than indicating a fault.

### Your subscription

Subscriptions are managed on the Stablepass website, not in the app. Sign in at app.stablepass.co and open Account to see the state of your subscription, update the card it is billed to, or cancel it.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end the period you have already paid for — access continues to the end of it.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the Me tab in the app, or by emailing hello@stablepass.co. What deletion removes and what must be kept is set out on the account deletion page.

### Something in the app looks wrong

If a video will not play, an update looks broken, or the app behaves unexpectedly, email hello@stablepass.co with your device model and iOS or Android version. A screenshot or screen recording is the single most useful thing you can attach.

## 3. What Stablepass can help with

Stablepass publishes updates from racing stables, and support covers the app and your account.

Questions about a particular horse — how it is going, what it will run in next, or buying a share in it — are for the stable concerned. Stablepass passes nothing on and cannot answer for a trainer.

## 4. Privacy and terms

The privacy policy and terms of service are linked in the footer of every page, and set out what Stablepass collects, why, and the rules for using the service.

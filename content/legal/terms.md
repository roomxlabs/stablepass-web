stablepass. is an entertainment and experience subscription. It does not sell shares in racehorses, syndicate interests, financial products, betting products, prize money rights or investment returns. Subscribers receive content access and racing experiences only.

## Use of stable content

Photos, videos and updates on stablepass. are shared by participating stables for subscribers only. You may not share, reproduce, screenshot, download or repost this content anywhere else, including on social media. Subscribers who do may have their subscription cancelled without refund.

## Billing and cancellation

New subscribers receive a free 30 day trial. After the trial the subscription is $19 per month, billed monthly. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

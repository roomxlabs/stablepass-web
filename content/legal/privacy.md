## What we collect

When you start a subscription or a free trial we collect your name, email address, phone number, year of birth and postcode. Year of birth and postcode are used only in aggregate, to understand the age and geographic spread of our subscribers.

## How we use it

We use your details to run your subscription, send the updates you have chosen to follow, and let you know about changes to the service. We do not sell your personal information.

## Your choices

You can update your details or close your account at any time from the Account section. You can turn off any notification type without leaving the service.

Stablepass Pty Ltd (“Stablepass”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal information when you use the Stablepass website, mobile application and related services (together, the “Platform”).

## 1. Scope and Commitment

Stablepass handles personal information responsibly and only for purposes permitted by law. Stablepass does not use personal information to provide financial product advice, arrange or deal in financial products, assess suitability, promote ownership interests, or operate any horse-racing syndicate, managed investment scheme or other regulated financial product.

This Policy applies to all Platform interactions and related communications.

## 2. Information We Collect

Stablepass may collect the following types of personal information:

- identity and contact details
- account credentials and authentication information
- subscription and transaction history
- support communications
- device, usage, log and security information
- User Content
- marketing preferences
- identity, business, licence or ownership-verification information, where reasonably necessary

Stablepass does not collect or store any financial information relating to ownership transactions between Trainers and Owners, including purchase amounts, contributions, bank details or settlement information.

Stablepass minimises collection to what is reasonably necessary for its functions.

## 3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected:

- directly from you when you create an account, subscribe, upload content, contact Stablepass, complete verification or use Platform features
- automatically through logs, cookies, SDKs and similar technologies
- from service providers or authorised third parties, where lawful

Stablepass does not use racing data or Platform usage data to infer personal characteristics, investment behaviour, financial preferences or suitability for ownership opportunities.

## 4. Purposes of Collection and Use

Stablepass may use personal information to:

- provide and manage accounts and subscriptions
- authenticate and secure accounts
- process payments (via third-party processors)
- provide support
- enable Trainer interactions
- operate, analyse and improve the Platform
- prevent fraud and misuse
- send service communications
- conduct marketing, where permitted
- comply with legal obligations

Stablepass does not use personal information to:

- evaluate, target, recommend or promote any ownership interest
- assess a User’s objectives, financial situation or needs
- determine eligibility or suitability to acquire any ownership interest
- facilitate, arrange or negotiate ownership transactions

## 5. Disclosures

Stablepass may disclose personal information to:

- payment processors
- authentication and messaging providers
- cloud hosting and IT providers
- analytics providers
- identity-verification providers
- other authorised Users where a feature requires it (e.g., messaging)
- regulators, ASIC, law enforcement or courts where lawful

Stablepass does not disclose personal information to Trainers for the purpose of arranging, negotiating, facilitating or promoting ownership transactions, nor for any financial-services purpose.

Stablepass does not sell or rent personal information.

## 6. Overseas Disclosures

Stablepass may disclose personal information to overseas recipients through its service providers. Before publication, Stablepass will identify the countries likely to receive information and assess cross-border disclosure obligations under the APPs.

## 7. Direct Marketing

Stablepass may send marketing communications where permitted by law and consistent with your preferences. All marketing messages include a functional unsubscribe method.

Service, security and transactional communications may still be sent where necessary.

## 8. Cookies, Analytics and Tracking

Stablepass may use cookies, SDKs, analytics tools, logs and similar technologies for:

- operation
- authentication
- preferences
- performance
- analytics
- security

The published policy and any consent mechanism will identify the actual tools and advertising or cross-site tracking practices used.

## 9. Security

Stablepass takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Measures may include:

- role-based access controls
- encryption
- secure authentication
- logging and monitoring
- supplier controls
- incident response processes

## 10. Retention and Deletion

Stablepass retains personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for:

- Platform operation
- legal obligations
- dispute resolution
- fraud prevention
- security
- legitimate business needs

Information is securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified when no longer required. Account deletion requests remain subject to lawful retention requirements.

## 11. Access and Correction

You may request:

- access to personal information Stablepass holds about you
- correction of inaccurate information

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

## 12. Privacy Complaints

Privacy enquiries or complaints should be sent to hello@stablepass.co. Stablepass will acknowledge, investigate and respond in accordance with applicable privacy law.

Individuals may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) where permitted.

## 13. Data Breaches

Stablepass maintains a data breach response process and will assess suspected eligible data breaches under applicable law. Where notification is required, Stablepass will notify the regulator and affected individuals.

## 14. Automated Decision-Making

If Stablepass uses personal information in automated decision-making that could reasonably be expected to significantly affect rights or interests, this Policy and relevant notices will be updated to describe the practice and applicable safeguards before the feature is used.
